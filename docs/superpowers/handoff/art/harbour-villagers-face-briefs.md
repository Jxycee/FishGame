# Villager face briefs (for the painted-face head texture)

Tone 2.1: grown-up goofy, dry, not cute. The faces are painted into the head texture (no stuck-on parts); the proxy heads are plain spheres of radius 0.23-0.27 m, face centre toward -Y (Unity +Z).
Family language: big white eyes with tiny dot pupils, heavy brows, a big soft nose, a slightly crooked mouth. Hair, hats, beards and glasses are separate geometry (see the manifest); everything below is paint.
