# RV There Yet? (UE 5.6, RG* C++) - mechanics notes for FishGame M5/M6

Method: CUE4Parse 1.2.2 mounts the paks (EGame GAME_UE5_6, 2 VFS, 19,101 files, Oodle via CUE4Parse's own downloader). Unversioned property VALUES are unreadable (no .usmap), but Blueprint function bytecode (Kismet) is versioned, so literals inside BP logic ARE readable. All below is paraphrased from bytecode/config/exe strings; nothing copied. Paths are under `Ride/Content/Ride/` unless noted. UE units are cm; I convert to m. Tags: [V] verified literal in bytecode/ini, [I] inference, [U] unverified peek at raw CDO bytes (no schema; position/type guessed). Rendered pseudo-code stays in `scratchpad/re/rv/tool/dumps/*.txt`.

## 1. Boat / water vehicle
- **No physical buoyancy.** `Boat/BP_Static_Boat` (landing craft, parent plain Actor) is a kinematic arcade boat. ARGWaterVehicle is only the jet-ski base (`Boat/BP_Static_JetSki`, parent RGWaterVehicle); it uses the same arcade logic plus a spring-damper only when airborne/off water.
- Gears -2..3 map to throttle alpha {-0.5, -0.25, 0, 0.33, 0.66, 1.0} [V]; jet ski gears -1..1 -> {-0.5, 0, 2.5} [V]. Throttle axis steps the gear (clamped -2..3); steering is an axis.
- speed = FInterpTo(speed, MaxSpeed*alpha, dt, rate 0.4); x(0.4..1.0) "forward clearance" (position sweep not blocked; jet ski 0.75..1); x SpeedCorrection = map(|turn|*|roll| 0..50 -> 1..0.6, interp 1) [V].
- Turn value chases the stick at rate 2; yaw += turn * speed*0.01*TurningMultiplier per tick, with no dt in the graph (frame-rate dependent: a bug to avoid) [V]. Lean: pitch +3.5 deg and roll +12 deg at full speed, scaled by speed/MaxSpeed (roll also by steering), interp 1.25 / 1 [V].
- Move: pos += (dir*speed + pushDir*PushCurve(serverTime-pushTime)*PushForce)*dt*FrameSpeed via SetActorLocation(sweep=true); Z chases the water plane (FInterpTo 5; falls at FallSpeed out of water) [V]. Defaults [U]: boat MaxSpeed 30 (first CDO double; matches the 0..30 map used for the push curve), jet ski doubles 20 / 5 / 900 (900 may be spring Stiffness).
- **Wave sampling**: `RGFunctionLibrary::QueryWaterInfo(WaterBodyComponent, pos, bool)` returns RGWaterQueryResult {WaterSurfaceLocation, WaterSurfaceNormal, WaterPlaneDepth, WaterSurfaceDepth, WaveInfo{Height, Normal, ReferenceTime, AttenuationFactor}, SplineInputKey, bIsInExclusionVolume} [V]. The boat samples **4 pontoon points** (Front/Back/Left/Right), averages normals and heights x HeightMultiplier, then rotates/offsets only the child `InteractiveMesh` (interp 8) [V]. Floating decor (`Boat/BP_Static_FloatingActor`) uses 1 sample; bob amplitude scales with BoatSize: map(1..15 m -> 1..0.1) [V].
- Jet ski off-water spring [V]: GravityOffset=980/k; damping=sqrt(k)*0.25; target z = water plane+10 cm; F = err*k - vz*damping. Shore check: sphere trace r=0.9 m ahead, tag `JetskiTraversable`; landscape hit zeroes throttle.
- In-water test: box overlap vs WaterBody, half-extents 10 x 6 x 1 m [V]. Hits on simulating bodies add 1,000,000 (cm-kg/s2) force along contact direction; boat-boat pushes clamp(speed,5,100); server collision event rate-limited; wave-landing SFX when speed>8, >0.5 s apart [V].
- **Riding (answer to "stand on a moving boat")**: two layers. (a) Seats: `Boat/Seat/BP_Boat_Seat_Driver` and `Interactables/TruckParts/BP_Seatable_TruckPart_BoatPassenger` are actors attached to the hull; server spawns 6 passenger seats at sockets PassengerSeat1..6; `Character.SitOnRideableActor(seat, sit)` attaches at the seat point +0.3 m up; Unmount input leaves [V]. (b) Free standing/walking is NOT scripted anywhere I found; the hull also has a ladder, a hatch with a crank button, and carries a whole RV on deck (BoxOverlap + SetIsVehicleOnBoat), so players walk on the deck [I]; the likely mechanism is UE CharacterMovement based-movement on the kinematically swept hull (free in UE, absent in Unity's CharacterController, hence the spec's TrySetParent). RGForceSeatComponent listens to its parent's begin-overlap and seats arriving players (`Character/Blueprints/GM_FirstPerson` TryToForceSeatPlayer); vehicle respawn seats the player in a random empty seat [V].
- Helm / authority: sitting in the driver seat makes the **client owner of the boat** (seat calls PlayerController `Server_SetActorOwner(boat, controller)`, cleared on leave) [V]. The seat forwards (steer, throttle) through interface `SetBoatTriggerActorInput`. Driver client runs the sim and calls reliable server RPC `Server Set Target Location and Rotation` every tick; everyone else (incl. server) VInterp/RInterp to the replicated target at speed 0.6 with a 2 cm dead-zone [V]. Native RGWaterVehicle adds NetSmoothingSpeed, NetSnapThreshold, NetRotationSnapThreshold, VehicleNetState [exe names].
- Boat push-model replicated set: IsThrottleDown, CurrentGear (RepNotify), TargetTurningDirectionValue (RepNotify), TargetBoatLocation/Rotation, IsInWater, IsBeingPushed, PushDirection, PushServerTime (peers evaluate the same push curve), BlockInput (RepNotify), TargetActor, ServerTimeWhenInitForcePushed, PushForce [V].
- Recovery tools: `Boat/ActorComponents/BPC_ForceBoatDirection_Volume` steers a boat toward a target actor: over 8 s direction lerps forward->toTarget and speed lerps input->MaxPushSpeed [V]; `Boat/Misc/BP_OutOfBoundsDetection_Volume`, `BP_DestroyBoat`; trucks use `IA_ResetVehicle` + ForceTeleport. Boat tick is disabled at `Significance.Low` (SignificanceExtension plugin) [V]. Boat roll is overwritten each tick from lean math, so a capsize case does not appear to exist [I]; trucks flip and use `Interactables/TruckFlipper/BP_Interactable_TruckFlipper` (impulse at a socket, 0.5 s delay).

## 2. Winch and rope
- Truck-mounted tool, not a player tug-of-war: `Vehicle/Blueprints/Winch/BP_Winch : RGWinch` is a child actor on the RV (front `BP_Winch`, rear `BP_RearWinch`). Replicated `WinchState {IsActive(lock), IsPulling, IsReleasing}`, `WinchHook`, `WinchRope` (all RepNotify). Rope = `DynamicRopeActor` (plugin DynamicRope) with GetRopeLength -> (Current, Target, Max); a CableComponent is the fallback [V].
- Co-op split [V]: one player carries the **hook** (`Interactables/Winch/BP_Interactable_WinchHook`, a pickup) to an anchor (piton/truck hierarchy); another holds the **remote** (`Interactables/Winch/BP_Interactable_WinchController`: nearest BP_VehicleBase, toggle front/rear) and presses Pull/Release/Lock; "Reset Winch" recalls the hook. HUD: `UI/PlayerHUD/WG_WinchBar_Rope(_Slider)` shows Current/Max, lock icon, direction arrows.
- All winch actions go through PlayerController server RPCs (`Server_ToogleWinching`, `Server_ResetWinchRope`, `Server_SetWinchControllerTarget`, `Server_SetWinchPole`) in `Character/Blueprints/BP_FirstPersonPlayerController` [V].
- **WinchStruggle** = a normalized 2D strain value (previous, current) event fired by native RGWinch, used for FX/audio like the gearbox-struggle event; not a minigame [V]. Remote-commander achievement counts at most 2 m of rope intake per update [V].
- Rope length, segments, pull force, struggle thresholds: **not reachable** (native defaults).

## 3. Proximity voice
- Config (`Ride/Config/DefaultEngine.ini`, `DefaultGame.ini`) [V]: `[Voice] bEnabled=true`, `VoiPSampleRate=Normal24000Hz`, `VoiPSoundClass=SC_VoiceChat`, `GameSession.bRequiresPushToTalk=true`, MaxPlayers=4.
- **Two voice paths**: `Character/Blueprints/BP_PlayerState` adds a UE `RGVOIPTalker` component (`Blueprints/BP_VOIPTalker`) only on **Steam builds**, with `Settings.AttenuationSettings = SA_VOIP` attached to the player root; non-Steam (EOS/Redpoint) builds use Redpoint's EOS voice-chat component and channel join in `BP_FirstPersonPlayerController.InitializeVOIP`, and crossplay sessions switch global voice features off [V].
- User settings (RGGameUserSettings) [exe]: VOIPMode {PushToTalk, ToggleToTalk, Auto(open mic, starts talking on noise gate)}, SoundVOIPVolume, MicInputGain, MicNoiseGateThreshold/Attack/Release, SilenceDetection threshold/attack/release, JitterBufferDelay, NetworkQuality. Controller RPCs ServerStartVOIP/ServerStopVOIP, FlushVOIPSettings, loopback toggle.
- `BP_VOIPTalker` on talking-begin sets the talker AudioComponent volume to VolumeMultiplier (**2.5** [U]) and sends it pre-effect at 1.0 to bus `Audio/Modulation/AudioBus/AB_WalkieTalkie_Routing` [V].
- Attenuation (`Audio/Attenuation/SA_*`, raw float bytes; shape fields guessed) [U]: SA_VOIP = full volume ~15 m, falloff ~300 m; SA_Equipment_WalkieTalkie 2 m / 40 m; SA_Player_Footsteps 1 m / 10 m; SA_Equipment_General 1 m / 20 m.
- `UI/PlayerHUD/WG_VoiceChatEntry` (speaker list): shows a talker when within MaxHearingDistance of the local player OR within MaxSpeakerSourceHearingDistance of any active walkie (`GameState.SpeakerSources`) [V logic]; the two CDO doubles are 200 m and 30 m but which is which is unconfirmed (order guessed) [U]. `Menu/WG_VoiceLevelWidget` = settings mic meter (preview capture, envelope follower, bar tinted by silence/noise thresholds, level x MicGain, two threshold ticks) [V].
- Walkie `Interactables/WalkieTalkie/BP_Interactable_WalkieTalkie` [V]: hold primary = transmit, release = stop; needs >=2 units; adds itself to replicated `RideGameState.SpeakerSources` via server RPC; AudioCapture component, press/release/end SFX; `RGOnlineFunctionLibrary::SetCurrentWalkieTalkieSpeaker(PlayerState)`. `RGWalkieTalkiePatch` exists only in the exe (log "WalkieTalkiePatch: Initializing...", no asset references), purpose unknown. `RGVoiceInputMeterSubsystem` also exe-only; no BP references it (the menu meter uses controller preview capture).

## 4. Hazards (server-authoritative; damage through PlayerController RPCs)
- **Shark** `Environment/Wildlife/Shark/*`: `RGShark` states Idle/Chase/Flee/Attack/Stranded; props detectPlayerDistance, attackRange, movementSpeed, turnSpeed, ShallowWaterThreshold, maxLengthFromStart, replicated CurrentSharkLocation [exe names; values not reachable]. `BP_SharkSpawner`: sphere trigger; when a player (server side) enters it rolls `SpawnChance >= rand(0,1)` (0.66 [U]) once, spawns `BP_Shark`, destroys itself; spawn point = navmesh random point, radius 26 m +0.5 m per ring, 6 rings [V]. Bite = `ServerSharkBite(shark, damageSettings)` on the victim's controller + multicast effect; `BP_Interactable_Shark` bites a random overlapping target after 1 s, 3 s cool-down; shark tick 0.12 s at Significance.Medium, off at Low; harpoon can drag it onto land (stranded) [V].
- **Piranha** `Environment/Wildlife/Piranha/*`: `BP_PiranhaJumper_01` (RGPiranhaJumper) is a cosmetic fish on a spline, advanced 0.015 per timer step every `JumpSpeed`, pause `DelayBetweenJump`; `BP_Interactable_Piranha` leech: **2 HP every 2 s**, ends after `timeUntilDone` ticks (10 [U] = 20 s, 20 HP total), 2 s attack delay, replicated IsBiting; jumper `Biting` RPC (reliable, server) starts it [V].
- **Snake** `Environment/Wildlife/Snake/*`: capsule trigger, random overlapping target, 1 s wind-up / 2 s recovery, `ServerSnakeBite`, damage type `DamageType.Player.Poison`; `Interactables/Antidote/BP_Interactable_SnakeAntidote` heals **40** poison damage [V].
- **Quicksand** (`Quicksand/BP_QuickSand : RGQuickSand`): overlap box; props CharacterPlaneSinkSpeed, VehiclePlaneSinkSpeed, MaxCharacterSinkDepth, MaxVehicleSinkDepth, DamagePerSecond, ProximityRange; character has replicated ActiveQuickSand, vehicle has SinkTime/SinkSpeed/MaxSinkRotation [exe names; numbers not reachable].
- **Breakables** (`Interactables/BP_BreakableActor : RGBreakableActor`, Chaos GeometryCollection): replicated `ReplicatedBrokenIndexes` (RepNotify) so late joiners rebuild; AllowedDamageTypes, Break/BreakWithVelocity [exe names].
- Zones: lava/radioactive tick every **1.5 s** (`Interactables/Hazards/BP_LavaZone`, `BP_RadioActiveZone`); cold water every **2 s** (`Hazards/BPC_ColdWaterPhysicsVolume_Damage_Effects`) [V]. Fall damage (`Character/Blueprints/BP_FirstPersonCharacter`): none until 12.5 m/s downward, then 10..100 HP linearly to 27.5 m/s [V].
- Leaks/bilge/bucket (spec 16.3/16.4): **no equivalent found** (only leak decals/VFX on a helicopter and gas).

## 5. Vehicle co-op roles, slop, congestion
- Truck (`Vehicle/Blueprints/BP_VehicleBase`, `BP_Vehicle_Winnebago_01`): driver *possesses* the pawn (Client_OnPossessed, multicast AttachDriver/DetachDriver); passengers sit in seat actors; shield+damage model with per-wheel damage tags (FL/FR/RL/RR) that detach wheels (multicast), RepairDamage; extra downward force in air = map(3..5 m height -> 0..-2900) [V]. `Plugins/AdvancedVehicleSystem/Content/AVS_Vehicle`: throttle/brake/steer RPCs unreliable, handbrake/shifter/engine reliable, inputs push-model replicated with RepNotify [V].
- Physics comedy: players throw other players (`Client_ThrowPlayer`): hold 0.2-1.0 s maps to 0-15 m/s, x(1..1.5) by strength, + 0.5 x thrower velocity [V]; ragdoll = replicated `IsRagdoll` RepNotify plus replicated bone transforms; revive = heal + un-ragdoll + move to safe spot with brief invulnerability; unseat on ragdoll [V]. Slide tuning exists (mMaxSlideAngle/Speed, friction per floor Default/Ice/Snow/DeepSnow).
- Physics props use networked-impulse RPCs and a client-authoritative owner with a sync interval (ServerApplyNetworkedImpulse, ServerSyncPhysicsState, ReleasePhysicsOwnership) [exe names]. Project physics (`DefaultEngine.ini`): substepping on, MaxSubstepDelta 0.02 s, MaxSubsteps 3, MaxPhysicsDelta 1/30 s [V].
- **RGNetworkCongestionSubsystem is UI-only**: tracks congested connections (log "Added congestion on server/client id", periodic GC); `UI/PlayerHUD/WG_NetworkSaturation` polls `IsAnyConnectionCongested` every UpdateInterval to show a bad-connection icon; `Menu/WG_OptionEntrySessionPlayer` uses `IsCongestedToPlayer(netId)` [V]. Nothing in gameplay is throttled by it.

## 6. Networking patterns for host-authoritative NGO
RV is host-listen UE replication with push-model properties (mark dirty only on change, flush dormancy before writes), and **all player-originated gameplay RPCs funnel through the PlayerController** (108 Server_* on `BP_FirstPersonPlayerController`), which maps to NGO `[Rpc(SendTo.Server)]` on the player object with server validation. Interactables use **temporary ownership transfer** (sitting sets the actor owner to the player's controller) so the occupant can send input, the same as the spec 16.2 owner-authority fallback; smoothing uses interp + snap thresholds, not rewinds. Time-driven motion replicates a **start server-time + curve** (PushServerTime/PushCurve, ServerStartTimeStamp) instead of streaming positions (use NGO ServerTime). Ambient hazards run server-only, with a significance/LOD component (tick 0.12 s, off when far) and compact replicated state (broken-piece index list, IsBiting, shark location). Continuous input unreliable, discrete events reliable (RV breaks its own rule by sending boat position reliably each tick). Net config: ConfiguredInternetSpeed 500000, MaxClientRate 200000, TotalNetBandwidth 600000, dynamic 4000..80000 B/s, connection timeout 40 s (initial 70 s), AES-GCM handler [V].

## Recommendations
1. Spec 16.2 Boat (M6 spike): add RV's approach as a spike alternative to try beside the NetworkRigidbody plan: kinematic script-driven hull (gear alpha table, speed chase 0.4, yaw proportional to speed, lean +3.5/+12 deg) with the 4-point `WaveMath.HeightAt` sample driving only a child visual (interp 8). It sidesteps rigidbody-parent jitter for passengers; keep NetworkRigidbody for cargo.
2. Spec 16.2 Helm (M6): RV's ownership handoff on sit/leave plus remote smoothing (interp 0.6, 2 cm dead-zone, snap thresholds) validates the owner-authority fallback; send helm input unreliably, not RV's reliable per-tick position RPC.
3. Spec 16.2 Passengers (M6): RV relies on engine based-movement for deck walking; for Unity keep TrySetParent walking as the acceptance path and add RV-style fixed seat anchors (sit = attach, +0.3 m, unmount key, random free seat after recovery) as an optional safe/fallback mode.
4. Spec 16.2 Recovery (M6): use RV's timed guided return (8 s lerp of heading to a target, speed lerp to a max push speed) as the capsized/stuck recovery effect rather than a hard teleport; fits the 10 s acceptance.
5. Spec 16.1 Voice (M6): RV voice is very wide [U] (full volume ~15 m, falloff ~300 m, speaker icons to ~200 m, 2.5x gain, PTT default with toggle/open-mic modes); spec 20 m audible is far tighter, so keep it tunable and add PTT/Toggle/Open-mic, mic gain, noise-gate threshold+attack+release, a Pause-menu level meter and nameplate speaker icons.
6. Spec 16.1 Walkie (M6, optional): hold-to-transmit item, needs 2 active units, registered in a replicated speaker-source list, voice routed through a filter bus with press/release/static SFX; cheap boat-to-dock comms.
7. Spec 15.4 Hazards (M5): ambush template = proximity sphere + one-shot server RNG gate (RV shark 0.66 [U]) + readable states (Idle/Chase/Flee/Attack/Stranded) + 1-2 s damage ticks; LOD-tick far AI (0.12 s mid, off far) for gull/raccoon/shark cadence.
8. Spec 15.4 Hazards (M5): give status hazards a cure item (RV poison 40 HP vs antidote; leech 2 HP/2 s for ~20 s [U]) - matches the "each hazard has an upside/counterplay" rule.
9. Spec 15.5 Downed/ragdoll (M5): RV fall damage starts at 12.5 m/s then 10..100 HP to 27.5 m/s; revive = heal + un-ragdoll + safe-spot teleport with brief invulnerability; thrown-player impulse 0-15 m/s + 0.5 x carrier velocity (spec ThrowMath is 4..14 m/s, +0.5 capped 7: already close).
10. Spec 15/16 polish (M5-M6): bad-connection icon from transport stats and a per-player "poor connection" mark in the session list (RV congestion subsystem is UI-only).
